Highlights

You independently built half of pstack. ponytail ≈ laziness-protocol / subtract-before-you-add; grill-with-docs ≈ interrogate; verification-recorder + evidence DBs ≈ prove-it-works; domain-modeling, tdd, prototype, diagnose all have pstack twins. High fit is everywhere — so the ranking is benefit-weighted: what matters is marginal value on top of your measured stack.
Three documented pain loops pstack directly targets. (1) Stale acceptance artifacts / proof-freshness resealing (model-benchmarking chore) → maintain-verification-skill. (2) Hand-rolled decision trails per repo (Run Records, review guides, handoffs) → show-me-your-work. (3) Manual transcript→skill retrospectives (code-review skill patched 137×) → reflect.
Biggest genuine gap: user-journey verification. Your verification is test/lint-centric. Paw consumer app, B2B portal, and course sites have no scripted "drive it like a user" check → create-verification-skill is the single highest-leverage install.
Do not double-inject styles. poteto-mode, laziness-protocol, subtract-before-you-add, minimize-reader-load, and no-comments duplicate or contradict your benchmarked stack (ponytail −10.3% cost, caveman −8.5%; rationale comments and ponytail: ceiling markers are load-bearing). Installing them adds the prompt weight you've been measurably cutting.
One observed incident argues for two skills. The Mnemopi silent-retention gap (07-19→08-10) → transcript-based recall as a hedge; the concurrent todo-state race under fan-out → principle-separate-before-serializing-shared-state as prevention.

Skip the duplication cluster. laziness-protocol / subtract-before-you-add / minimize-reader-load / no-comments / poteto-mode. You already run measured equivalents; these only add back prompt weight you benchmarked away.
Method note. pstack's philosophy is already your philosophy — you converged on it independently and instrumented it. That is why the ranking is benefit-weighted: fit alone would put nearly everything at the top and tell you nothing. Follow your own doctrine: install Tier 1, measure adherence/cost like you did with ponytail and the cadence-governor, and delete what doesn't earn its tokens.
